The idea

const elapsed = Date.now() - start;

Nothing is ever incremented and nothing animates toward a value. There is no accumulator, so there is no first frame showing zero, no drift, and no catch-up after the tab was hidden. Miss a thousand frames and the next one is still exactly right.

Which is the whole difference between a clock and a tally. A tally that counts up from zero is briefly wrong — every frame before the last shows a figure that was never true. A clock computed from its epoch has no wrong state to catch.
